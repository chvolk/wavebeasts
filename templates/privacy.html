Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

WaveBeasts is a scan-to-creature game. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. The short version: we collect only what the game needs to work, we don't run ads or third-party trackers, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account info - the username you choose and a securely hashed password. We don't require an email or real name.
  • Game data - the beasts you catch, your currency and items, your buddy, your battle/trade activity, and the devices ("nodes") you register (each with a random token you create).
  • Scan snapshots - when a device you've linked submits a scan, it sends a bundle of real-world signal readings (e.g. nearby Wi-Fi/Bluetooth identifiers, sensor values). We use these to generate a beast on the server. We store a small record of the outcome (what was generated) rather than a long-term log of your raw environment.
  • Basic technical data - standard server request logs (IP address, timestamp) kept briefly for security and reliability.

On your device

The app and the standalone engine can run and generate beasts locally, offline, with no account. The phone app reads device sensors (camera, location, etc.) only with your permission and only to build a scan. That data stays on your device unless you link an account and choose to submit a snapshot or upload your local collection.

How we use it

  • To run the game: generate beasts, keep your collection, power battles, trades and your buddy.
  • To keep the service secure and working (anti-cheat, rate limits, abuse prevention).

We do not sell or rent your data, we do not show ads, and we don't share your data with third parties except infrastructure providers that host the service on our behalf (e.g. our hosting and database provider), under their own safeguards.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies - a session cookie to keep you signed in and a security (CSRF) cookie. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The site works with just these.

Retention & your choices

  • We keep your data while your account is active.
  • You can release beasts, delete nodes, and request account deletion - which removes your account and associated game data.

Children

WaveBeasts isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy as the game evolves; we'll change the date above. Questions, data access, or a deletion request? Email jwest@jameswest.space.

This is a plain-language policy for a hobby/beta service and is provided for transparency, not as legal advice.
