Welcome to WaveBeasts

Scan the real world into collectible creatures. Here's the loop:

1 · Get a scanner

Play free right now - install the app or run the engine on a Pi from the Download page. Local play needs no account.

2 · Link a node

Create a node in Nodes, paste its token into your scanner, and its scans feed your account here. Free accounts get one node.

3 · Catch, battle, bond

Scans yield beasts (sometimes a battle first). Premium unlocks trading, battles, the async ladder, a companion Buddy, and up to 12 nodes.
