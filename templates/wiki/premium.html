Verified vs imported beasts

Account snapshots are generated on the server and produce verified beasts on both tiers. Importing a local collection is Premium and keeps those beasts unverified; they can be collected and used as personal companions, but not traded or entered into competitive teams.

Trading safely

An offer can contain up to three beasts plus shards and cores. Currency is held until the offer resolves. Declining, withdrawing, or closing a listing returns the held currency. A beast in an active offer cannot also be listed or released. Trading a beast removes it from the former owner’s team and Buddy slot.
