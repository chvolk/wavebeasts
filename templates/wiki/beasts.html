Beasts & types

There are nine elements:

embertideleaf sparkstonegale frostshadelumen

Every beast has one type, with a 35% chance of a second (dual) type. Shade and lumen are a mirror pair - each beats the other and resists itself - a light/dark axis orthogonal to the elemental wheel.

Rarity & shinies

Five tiers, weighted by effective entropy - higher entropy shifts the odds toward the top:

commonuncommon rare epic legendary

Rarity also sets a minimum IV floor (better guaranteed stats) and a catch resistance (harder to catch):

RarityIV floor (of 31)Catch resistance

Shinies are a rare alternate colouring, more likely at high entropy. The exact odds are intentionally secret - treat them as very rare.

Natures

Every individual carries one of 16 natures. They're personality flavour - they do not change stats; apps use them to theme behaviour (e.g. your Buddy's chat tone).

Brave · Timid · Playful · Grumpy · Loyal · Curious · Lazy · Fierce · Gentle · Clever · Wild · Cheerful · Stoic · Mischievous · Bold · Shy

Tribes & stats

Beasts have six stats: HP, ATK, DEF, SPD, SP, RES (base values 45-80). Each beast belongs to a tribe that biases two of those stats (+18):

TribeFavours
brawlerATK, HP
wardenDEF, RES
skirmisherSPD, ATK
channelerSP, RES
colossusHP, DEF
tricksterSPD, SP

Stats scale with level (1->100). XP to the next level is level³ × 15. Beasts evolve through 5 forms at levels 1 / 16 / 32 / 48 / 64, with stat multipliers 1.0 -> 1.25 -> 1.55 -> 1.9 -> 2.3.

Tribe passives (e.g. crit or damage perks) are defined but not yet applied in battle - they're coming in a later update.
