Battle mechanics

Battles are 3-v-3 auto-battles and are fully deterministic from a seed. Your team is up to three fighters; in a local scan your "party" is your three highest-level beasts.

  • Turn order: the higher SPD fighter acts first each round (ties favour the challenger's side). Up to 300 turns.
  • Move: a random damaging move; a beast with none uses Struggle (power 35).
  • Damage: ((2×level/5 + 2) × power × ATK / DEF) / 50 + 2, then modifiers below. Physical moves use ATK vs DEF; special moves use SP vs RES.
  • STAB ×1.5 when the move's type matches the attacker's type.
  • × type effectiveness (see the type chart).
  • Crit: 6.25% chance for ×1.5 damage.
  • Spread: every hit rolls ×0.85-1.00. Minimum damage is 1.

A side wins when the other is wiped; at the turn cap the win goes to whoever has more total HP remaining (equal -> draw). Battle on scan: once you have a party, a wild encounter has a 35% chance to challenge you - win and it's weakened and catchable; lose and it flees.
