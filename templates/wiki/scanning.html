How it works

A scan bundles the signals a device can read into a ScanBundle and submits it. The server distills two numbers from it:

  • Entropy - how much genuine, varied information the reading carries.
  • Coherence (0.4-1.0) - whether the signals reinforce or cancel. Aligned signals reinforce; opposed phases, heavy motion, and saturated signals cancel.

The effective entropy that drives every roll is entropy × coherence - so more data is not a guaranteed win. Each scan resolves to one of three outcomes:

  • Beast - a creature appears. This needs an identity signal (QR > barcode > NFC > image features, or a derived identity from ambient Wi-Fi/sensors). Chance rises with entropy.
  • Resource - currency, food, or a drive (see below).
  • Nothing - even a good reading can collapse to nothing via destructive interference.

For account play, stats are rolled server-side with a per-scan secret nonce, so clients do not choose their account beasts’ stats. The species is deterministic from the bundle; the individual is not.

Scan in your browser · Premium

Open Scan to submit manually to your signed-in account. Type one code, scan a QR/barcode, or capture/choose an image. A new camera input replaces the previous one.

Camera permission is requested only when you open it. Photos are reduced to a perceptual hash and a six-color palette on your device; no photo is uploaded. Optional orientation readings use your browser’s real sensors where supported. Denied or unavailable sensors are skipped.

Scans share one browser-node cooldown across tabs. There is no background or automatic scan mode on the website. For ongoing sensing, use a node or app. Catch queued discoveries from your Beastiary with drives from the same account inventory.
