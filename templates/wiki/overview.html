One game, two ways to play

Local play: the Go engine keeps your collection in a SQLite database on your device. Its browser GUI is also the interface inside the standalone mobile app.

Account play: devices submit scans to wavebeasts.com. The server rolls and stores your online beasts, wallet and inventory. Link a node code in the app’s World tab to see your account identity and send snapshots.

These collections are separate. Premium can import local catches into your account as unverified collection items. Only server-generated, verified beasts can trade or compete.
