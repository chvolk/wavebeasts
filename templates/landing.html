Turn the world into monsters.

WaveBeasts is a scan-to-creature game. Point sensors at the real world — a barcode, a QR code, a photo, WiFi, Bluetooth, RF, temperature, light — and generate collectible WaveBeasts. The richer your sensor sweep, the rarer the catch.

Run the engine anywhere (a Raspberry Pi, a laptop, your Android phone) and link listener nodes to your account: each node feeds rate-limited sensor snapshots to your profile, so your collection grows even while you go about your day. Trade and battle other trainers.

How it works

1. Create an account. 2. Add a node (your phone, a Pi, EDI/Bishop, or any PC running the WaveBeast engine). 3. The node uploads sensor snapshots on a timer — speed it up by spending in-game currency. 4. Catch, collect, trade, and battle.
