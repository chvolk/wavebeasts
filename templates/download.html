Then
Open http://localhost:8777 and play. Scans yield about once every five minutes. To grow your online beastiary, create a node in your profile, paste its token into the app's WORLD tab, and submit snapshots or upload your local catches. Source & releases on GitHub.
Build on it
WaveBeast has no client lock-in: anything that can produce signals can feed it. Wire your own tools in over the HTTP API or CLI on desktop/Linux, or share text / wavebeast:// deep links into the Android app. See the integration guide.
